Every time Meme Xray gives a coin a research verdict, the call gets logged to a server ledger at that moment — label, score, market cap, all timestamped before anyone knows what happens next. 48 hours later, a scheduled job checks the coin's real market cap on DexScreener and records what actually happened. Nothing on this page is curated after the fact.

Methodology: a coin is logged once per open research verdict (not re-logged while a call is still pending). "Dumped" means the market cap fell 50%+ (or the pair could no longer be found at all, which is the closest signal this data source has to a rug or delisting) within the 48h window. "Held" means it didn't. Calls with less than 48h of history simply aren't counted yet — there's no way to shortcut that wait without guessing.
